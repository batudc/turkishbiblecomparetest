ca. 5-4 BC — Birth of Jesus
Jesus is born in Bethlehem during the reign of King Herod
Matthew traces Jesus' genealogy and records the visit of the Magi from the East, establishing Jesus as the promised Messiah from the line of David.
ca. 26-27 AD — Beginning of Ministry
Jesus is baptized by John and tempted in the wilderness
Jesus is identified as God's Son and declares the kingdom of heaven is at hand. He gathers his first disciples (Peter, Andrew, James, and John).
ca. 26-30 AD — Public Ministry
Jesus teaches and performs miracles throughout Judea and Galilee
Jesus delivers the Sermon on the Mount, performs miracles demonstrating his authority, and prepares his disciples through teaching. The kingdom of heaven is manifested through his works.
ca. 30 AD — Death & Resurrection
Jesus is crucified and rises from the dead on the third day
Jesus fulfills the prophecies of his suffering and establishes his kingdom through his resurrection. He commissions his disciples to make disciples of all nations.
