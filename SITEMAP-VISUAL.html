🚀 Deployment & Caching
Vercel: Hosts all HTML files directly (no server-side redirects for living pages). Service Worker (sw.js) handles offline caching.

Service Worker: Precaches app shell (v4), caches translation data (v1) on first read.

Browser Cache: May be stale after updates. Users can hard-refresh (Cmd+Shift+R / Ctrl+Shift+R) to clear.
